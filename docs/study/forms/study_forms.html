MedLedger AI – study forms

Printable forms for Phase 11. Print one set per participant in the language they choose.

  1. Information and consent – English, Nepali, Hindi
  2. Patient task sheet – English, Nepali, Hindi
  3. Clinician task sheet – English
  4. Sample report for the tasks (fictional)
  5. Questionnaire (SUS) – English, Nepali, Hindi
  6. Observer's task log
  7. Checklist for collecting real reports

The Nepali and Hindi texts are draft translations. Before the study, have a native speaker check them, ideally by translating them back into English and comparing. Enter results in the CSV files under docs/study/templates (copied to docs/study/data).

SUS items: J. Brooke, “SUS: a quick and dirty usability scale,” 1996 (“system” replaced by “MedLedger”).

Information and consent

You are invited to try MedLedger, a website that keeps medical reports safe and explains them in simple language. The study is part of a university research project.

You will do a few short tasks on a phone or computer (upload a sample report, share it with a doctor, stop sharing, read the explanation) while a researcher watches, and then fill in a short questionnaire. It takes about 30 minutes.

You will use a test account and a made-up sample report. Please do not enter any real medical information.

Taking part is your choice. You can stop at any time without giving a reason, and your answers will then be deleted.

We record only your participant number, age group, the tasks you completed, the time taken and your answers. We do not record your name with your answers. Results are published only as totals.

जानकारी और सहमति

आपको MedLedger आज़माने के लिए आमंत्रित किया जाता है। यह वेबसाइट मेडिकल रिपोर्ट सुरक्षित रखती है और उन्हें आसान भाषा में समझाती है। यह अध्ययन एक विश्वविद्यालय के शोध प्रोजेक्ट का हिस्सा है।

आप फ़ोन या कंप्यूटर पर कुछ छोटे काम करेंगे (नमूना रिपोर्ट अपलोड करना, डॉक्टर के साथ साझा करना, साझा करना बंद करना, व्याख्या पढ़ना)। शोधकर्ता देखेंगे, फिर आप एक छोटी प्रश्नावली भरेंगे। इसमें लगभग 30 मिनट लगते हैं।

आप एक परीक्षण खाते और एक काल्पनिक नमूना रिपोर्ट का उपयोग करेंगे। कृपया कोई असली स्वास्थ्य जानकारी न डालें।

भाग लेना आपकी इच्छा है। आप किसी भी समय बिना कारण बताए रुक सकते हैं, और तब आपके उत्तर मिटा दिए जाएँगे।

हम केवल आपकी प्रतिभागी संख्या, आयु वर्ग, पूरे किए गए काम, लगा समय और आपके उत्तर रखते हैं। आपका नाम उत्तरों के साथ नहीं रखा जाता। परिणाम केवल कुल संख्या के रूप में प्रकाशित होते हैं।

Checklist for collecting real reports

  1. Written approval from your guide / the institution's ethics committee, and written permission from each partner laboratory.
  2. Collect 50–100 reports: at least 30 printed (scanned or photographed flat), at least 20 phone photos, and any digital PDFs. Mix laboratories and test panels.
  3. Anonymise before the files leave the laboratory: black out (on paper, then photograph) or crop the patient name, relatives, phone, address, ID numbers, dates of birth, barcodes/QR codes, and doctors' names and signatures. Keep ages and sex.
  4. Name the files R001, R002 … (no names in file names) and put them in docs/study/data/reports/. Never commit them to a public repository.
  5. Label every printed value in labels.csv exactly as printed (name, value, unit, range, H/L flag). A second person checks 10% of the rows.
  6. Run eval_real_reports.ts. If it reports possible personal details, check those files again.
  7. Build the review pack (make_review_pack.ts) and give it to two doctors separately.
